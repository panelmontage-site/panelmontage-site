What Affects the Price

One Square Metre Does Not Tell the Whole Story

Two buildings with the same floor area may have significantly different installation costs. A universal rate without understanding the project can therefore produce an overly approximate result.

Costs depend not only on quantities, but also on building height, configuration, connection complexity, site conditions, material lifting arrangements and the region where the work will be performed.

This is why we first collect the project information and then determine the scope of the estimate.

Factors Considered Include:

✓ Type and quantity of installation work
✓ Building area and geometry
✓ Working height
✓ Complexity of junctions and connections
✓ Readiness of load-bearing structures
✓ Need for lifting equipment
✓ Construction site conditions
✓ Project location

Before Work Begins

The Estimate Must Reflect Actual Site Conditions

A preliminary price given over the phone or based solely on area does not always reflect actual installation conditions.

Before the final scope of work is agreed, additional documents, clarification of technical solutions or an inspection of the building's actual condition may be required.

Why This Matters

If working height, frame readiness, connection complexity, equipment access, logistics and actual work quantities are not considered in advance, the initial figure may differ significantly from the actual cost of carrying out the work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Installation Cost Estimation

Can I request installation work only, without purchasing materials?

Yes. If the materials have already been purchased, the required installation work can be considered separately. Information about the building, materials and site conditions will be needed.

Can sandwich panel installation costs be calculated based on area?

Area is one of the initial parameters, but the final cost also depends on working height, panel type, building geometry, connections, frame readiness, lifting equipment and other site-specific conditions.

What if the exact work quantities are unknown?

Submit any available drawings, dimensions or design documents. After reviewing them, we can determine which additional information is required.

Can materials and installation be estimated together?

Yes. The required materials, their supply and the corresponding installation works can be considered together where necessary.

Do you work on projects in other regions?

PANEL MONTAGE considers projects throughout Kazakhstan. The feasibility and conditions of carrying out the work depend on the specific project.

Can I submit documents through the website?

Yes. Use the project estimation page to submit the basic information and available project materials.
