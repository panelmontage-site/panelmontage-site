Installation of steel structures and frames for warehouses, hangars, industrial, logistics and commercial buildings. The scope and cost are calculated from the project, drawings, quantities and conditions of the specific construction site.

Steel Frame

The Building Structure Must Match the Project

Steel structures form the load-bearing framework of many warehouse, industrial and prefabricated buildings. The accuracy of frame installation affects the correct installation of subsequent building elements.

For this reason, the cost and organization of installation work are not determined by the total steel weight alone. Building geometry, element types, height, connections, site conditions, installation sequence and lifting equipment requirements must also be considered.

If the project has not yet been prepared, work can begin with a concept design or project design development .

Next Stage

The Steel Frame Is Connected with the Walls, Roofing and Facade

For a prefabricated building, steel frame installation is usually not an isolated task. Once the frame is complete, the building envelope is formed.

Therefore, even at the calculation stage, it is useful to understand which wall and roofing materials are specified by the project, which junctions will be required and how subsequent work will be organized.

If required, PANEL MONTAGE can separately consider material selection and supply and sandwich panel installation .

Questions

Steel Structure Installation

Can steel structure installation be calculated from a completed project?

Yes. For the calculation, you can send the project, drawings, element schedules and other available documents. The project location and construction site conditions are also taken into account.

Can I contact you if there is no project yet?

Yes. If you only have an idea, intended use and approximate building dimensions, you can begin with a concept design or determine whether project development is required.

What affects the cost of steel structure installation?

The calculation is affected by the scope and type of structures, building height, project connections, condition of the foundation, lifting equipment requirements, site access, region and other project conditions.

Can I order installation only if the steel structures have already been fabricated?

This option can be considered. For assessment, the project documentation, available structures, completeness of the supplied elements and site conditions need to be reviewed.

Can the steel frame and sandwich panels be calculated together?

Yes. If required, steel structure installation, panel selection and supply, and subsequent installation of the building envelope can be considered separately within the same project.

Do you work in regions across Kazakhstan?

PANEL MONTAGE considers projects in different regions of Kazakhstan. The possibility and conditions of carrying out the work are determined for each specific project.
