From Production Requirements

The Building Must Match the Future Production Process

For a production facility, defining only the building area is not enough. The layout and structural solutions are influenced by the type of production, equipment placement, internal height, technological passages, gates, engineering systems and the organization of internal processes.

It is therefore reasonable to begin by understanding the technological requirements of the facility and then proceed to a concept design , project development and calculation.

If the project already exists, it is possible to proceed directly to calculating materials, steel structures and installation works.

Cost

The Cost of an Industrial Building Cannot Be Determined by Floor Area Alone

The same floor area does not mean the same project cost. A production workshop can differ significantly from a conventional warehouse in height, spans, steel consumption, engineering systems and requirements for the premises.

The preliminary budget is therefore prepared from specific initial data, and the calculation is refined as the project becomes more detailed.

Engineering Systems

Engineering Solutions Depend on the Production Process

Production premises have specific requirements for air exchange, temperature conditions and the placement of engineering systems.

Ventilation and air conditioning should therefore preferably be considered before the building is completed, at a stage when the main facility parameters can still be coordinated with the structural solutions.

For each facility, the scope of engineering solutions is determined by the project, the purpose of the premises and applicable requirements.

FAQ

Industrial Buildings and Production Facilities

Can I contact you if the industrial project has not yet been developed?

Yes. You can begin with a description of the future production process, approximate floor area, height, site and basic requirements. It is then possible to determine what initial information and level of project development are required.

Can you calculate a facility based on a completed project?

Yes. The project, drawings, specifications and schedules can be used to calculate materials and the corresponding installation works.

Can steel structure installation be ordered separately?

Yes. If the necessary initial data are available, installation of the steel frame can be considered separately.

Can I order only sandwich panel installation?

Yes. Installation of the building envelope can be considered as a separate stage if the frame and project solutions have already been prepared.

Can materials be selected for an industrial facility?

Yes. After reviewing the project or initial parameters, the selection of panels, facade materials, components, fasteners and other required items can be considered.

Do you work with ventilation for production premises?

This direction is considered separately according to the purpose of the premises, production processes and the project's technical requirements.

Do you work only in Almaty?

No. PANEL MONTAGE considers industrial projects in different regions of Kazakhstan. Logistics and work conditions are determined for each specific facility.
