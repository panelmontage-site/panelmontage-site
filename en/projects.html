Our Approach

Every Project Begins with Different Initial Information

One client may need to build a warehouse but only have approximate dimensions. Another may already have a completed design and need to determine the materials and installation costs.

At a third site, the steel frame may already be installed, while wall and roof panels, facade solutions or engineering systems still need to be selected.

That is why PANEL MONTAGE does not begin by selling a particular material. We first establish the current stage of the facility and identify the next task to be addressed.

Where Can a Project Start?

01 Only a building concept is available.
02 The dimensions and intended use are known.
03 A sketch or completed design is available.

Three Scenarios

What Stage Is Your Project At?

SCENARIO 01

You Only Have an Idea

You know the intended use of the future facility, its approximate size or the available land plot, but do not yet have a completed design. The process begins with collecting initial information and preparing a preliminary estimate.

SCENARIO 02

You Already Have a Design

Work can move on to reviewing quantities, materials, supply requirements, costs and the organization of installation works.

SCENARIO 03

Construction Is Already Underway

If part of the facility has already been built, the next step is to determine which materials and works are required for the following stage.

Engineering Solutions

A Building Is More Than Its Frame and Panels

Depending on its intended use, a facility may require ventilation, air conditioning, cold storage rooms or specialized construction solutions.

Engineering requirements should therefore be considered before the building envelope is completed, while penetrations, equipment locations and connections can still be coordinated with the building structure.
