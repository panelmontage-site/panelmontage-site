Warehouse Cost

The Price Depends on the Parameters of the Specific Project

A single universal price per square meter is not enough for an accurate warehouse calculation. Two buildings with the same floor area can differ significantly in structural solutions and scope of work.

The cost is affected by spans, building height, steel consumption, type of building envelope, gates and openings, roofing system, engineering solutions, logistics and site conditions.

For this reason, it is usually better to first obtain a construction cost estimate and then refine individual items.

FAQ

Warehouses and Warehouse Buildings

Can I contact you if I only have an idea for a warehouse?

Yes. To begin, it is enough to describe the intended use of the facility, approximate dimensions, region and basic requirements. From there, it is possible to determine what initial information and level of design development are required.

Can you calculate a warehouse based on an existing project?

Yes. Project documentation, drawings, specifications and schedules can be used to calculate materials and the corresponding installation works.

What determines the cost of warehouse construction?

The cost depends on the building dimensions and height, structural system, steel structures, type of building envelope, roofing, openings, engineering solutions, location and site conditions.

Can I order steel structure installation separately?

Yes. If the project and structures are already prepared, the required scope of installation work can be considered separately.

Can sandwich panels and installation be arranged together?

Yes. Materials, supply and installation can be considered within one project. The specific scope is determined from the project documentation and initial data.

Can storage racking be selected for a future warehouse?

Yes. Storage systems are best considered with regard to the dimensions of the premises, layout, aisles, handling equipment and the type of goods being stored.

Does PANEL MONTAGE work only in Almaty?

No. Projects are considered in different regions of Kazakhstan. Installation, supply and logistics conditions are determined for each specific project.
