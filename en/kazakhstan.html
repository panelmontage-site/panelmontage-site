Geographic Coverage

Every Project Has Its Own Construction Location

Working across Kazakhstan means that an estimate must take into account not only the building area or the number of panels, but also the location of the construction site.

The region affects material logistics, mobilization arrangements, delivery schedules, equipment availability and the conditions under which work can be performed.

This is why an enquiry begins with the city or district, the intended use of the building, its dimensions and its current stage of completion.

Information for a Regional Estimate

01 Region, city or district where the project is located.
02 Intended use of the building.
03 Dimensions, area and height.
04 Project documentation, sketches or photographs.
05 Materials already available.
06 Types of work required.

Cost

Location Is Only One Factor in Cost Estimation

A project's cost cannot be accurately determined solely by the city name or a price per square metre.

The final amount depends on building type, dimensions, height, structural design, materials, installation work quantities, site conditions, logistics and the equipment required.

For this reason, we first collect the initial information for a regional project and then estimate the specific scope of work.

Factors Affecting the Estimate

01 Building area and geometry.
02 Type of materials.
03 Installation height.
04 Number of junctions and connections.
05 Equipment required.
06 Logistics and site conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

PANEL MONTAGE Services in Kazakhstan's Regions

Do you operate only in Almaty?

No. We consider enquiries for projects in different regions of Kazakhstan. The exact project location is required for estimation.

Can I obtain a project estimate remotely for another city?

A preliminary assessment can begin with dimensions, project documentation, drawings, photographs and a description of the required work. Whether a further site inspection is necessary depends on the specific project.

Is material delivery included in the calculation?

If material supply is included in the proposed scope, logistics are assessed according to the specific region, materials and project.

Can I order installation services only?

Yes. An enquiry may cover only the agreed type of installation work. Before estimation, it is important to establish which materials the customer already has and whether the building is ready for installation.

Can I order materials together with installation?

For a specific project, material selection, procurement coordination, supply arrangements and subsequent installation work can be considered as a connected scope.

What should I submit for an initial estimate?

Specify the city or district, building purpose, main dimensions and required work. Attach any available project documents, sketches, photographs or other relevant materials.
