About Us

What Is PANEL MONTAGE?

PANEL MONTAGE focuses on construction and engineering solutions for industrial, warehouse, logistics and commercial facilities throughout Kazakhstan.

Work can begin at different stages: when a client has only an idea, the dimensions of a proposed building, completed design documentation or an existing steel frame that requires materials and installation.

The purpose of PANEL MONTAGE is to connect the individual elements of a project: initial information, estimation, structural solutions, materials, supply, engineering systems and installation works.

Practical Approach

Materials Are Evaluated Based on Their Actual Use on Site

Selecting a construction material should not be reduced to its price per square meter. Installation also depends on geometry, completeness of supply, packaging, surface condition, connections and suitability for the intended application.

Practical experience in construction and installation makes it possible to evaluate materials in terms of their subsequent use, not merely their purchase.

That is why PANEL MONTAGE considers material selection, quantity estimation and installation as interconnected processes.

Principles

The Principles Behind the PANEL MONTAGE Approach

Understand the Facility First

Pricing and technical solutions can only be properly assessed once the facility's purpose, dimensions, location, design and actual condition are understood.

Consider Materials and Installation Together

A material must be suitable not only on a commercial quotation, but also during delivery, layout, fastening and subsequent operation.

Avoid Promises Without Project Information

Costs, schedules and technical specifications depend on the particular facility. Information is therefore collected first, and a proposed solution follows.

One Facility, Interconnected Requirements

The steel frame, panels, facade, engineering systems, materials and installation all affect one another. These relationships are considered when developing a solution.
