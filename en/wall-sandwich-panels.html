Building Envelope

A Building Wall Is More Than Just the Panel Area

Wall sandwich panels form the external or internal building envelope.

A proper calculation should consider not only the total wall area, but also the purpose of the building, building height, openings, supporting frame arrangement, junction details, fastening and the required flashing and finishing elements.

If the panels have not yet been purchased, material selection, project-specific components and installation can be considered together. If the panels are already on site, the required installation work can be calculated separately.

Panel Selection

Which Parameters Matter

A wall sandwich panel cannot be selected correctly based on a single parameter. Its characteristics should be considered together.

01

Thickness

Selected according to the building's purpose, operating conditions and project requirements.

02

Insulation Core

The type of insulation core affects the properties and intended application of the panel.

03

Coating

The protective and decorative coating is selected according to operating conditions.

04

Geometry

Length, width, profile and manufacturing accuracy are important during installation.

05

Joint System

The connection design affects panel joints and installation of the corresponding detail.

06

Fastening

The type and position of fasteners depend on the panel, supporting structure and project solution.

07

Openings

Gates, windows and doors require separate detailing.

08

Junctions

Corners, plinth areas, parapets and connections require appropriate construction details.

Material Selection

The Cheapest Panel Does Not Always Mean a Lower Total Project Cost

When comparing offers, it is important to consider more than the price per square meter of the panel itself.

The final project package can also be affected by panel dimensions, the number of joints, flashing and finishing elements, fasteners, sealing materials, delivery, unloading and installation requirements.

For this reason, it is more practical to evaluate the complete solution for a specific building rather than a single product item.

Panels and Components

A complete wall system may require not only sandwich panels, but also fasteners, flashing elements, sealing materials and components for openings and junctions.

The required package depends on the building, frame configuration, panel layout and the construction details specified for the project.

Cost

What Determines the Cost of Wall Sandwich Panels and Installation

The final cost cannot be determined correctly from the wall area alone.

The calculation may depend on panel characteristics, building height, number of openings, installation complexity, condition of the supporting frame, required flashing elements, access to the work area and the location of the project.

If materials are also required, the scope of supply, logistics and related components are considered separately.

Questions

Wall Sandwich Panels: Frequently Asked Questions

Can wall sandwich panels be ordered without installation?

Yes. Depending on the project, material selection and supply can be considered separately from installation work.

Can you calculate both the panels and installation?

Yes. If sufficient initial data is available, the required materials, components and installation scope can be considered within one project.

What if the sandwich panels have already been purchased?

In this case, the installation task can be reviewed separately. Information about the panels, building frame, dimensions, openings and project location will be useful for the calculation.

How is the required panel thickness selected?

Panel thickness should be considered together with the purpose of the building, operating conditions and project requirements. It should not be selected solely by comparing prices.

Do openings affect the installation calculation?

Yes. Gates, doors, windows and other openings affect panel layout, junction details, flashing elements and the scope of installation work.

Do you work outside Almaty?

PANEL MONTAGE considers projects in different regions of Kazakhstan. The conditions depend on the project location, scope of work, materials and logistics.
