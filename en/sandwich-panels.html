Building Envelope System

A Sandwich Panel Is More Than a Sheet Attached to a Steel Frame

The final result depends on a combination of several elements: the project, readiness of the steel frame, panel specifications, material handling, fasteners, sealing, junctions and flashing elements.

Therefore, when preparing for installation, it is important to consider not only the total panel area, but also the actual geometry of the building, its height, openings, roof, construction details, lifting conditions and the actual readiness of the supporting structure.

PANEL MONTAGE can become involved both in projects where the panels have already been purchased and at an earlier stage when materials need to be selected, quantities calculated and the scope of work defined.

Material Selection

Panels Should Be Selected According to the Building Requirements

The same type of sandwich panel is not a universal solution for every building. Selection depends on the intended use of the facility, required temperature conditions, building structure, project requirements and operating conditions.

The selection process considers the panel type, intended application, insulation core, thickness, external coating, geometry and required accessories.

PANEL MONTAGE works with a network of verified manufacturers and suppliers. Where required, panel selection and supply can therefore be considered together with the installation requirements of the project.

Installation Practice

Problems Often Arise from the Installation System, Not the Panel Itself

Problems may be related to preparation of the supporting structure, material handling, fastening, joints, junctions, flashing elements or poor coordination between different stages of work.

Therefore, it is important to assess the complete system rather than the panel alone: the steel frame, materials, fasteners, construction details and the quality of installation operations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Sandwich Panel Installation

How much does sandwich panel installation cost per m²?

The cost depends on the panel type, total area, building height, number of openings, complexity of construction details, steel frame readiness, material lifting conditions and project region. Therefore, the price is calculated after the initial project information has been received. For more information, see our sandwich panel installation cost page .

What is the difference between installing wall and roof sandwich panels?

They are different parts of the building envelope system. They differ in function, position, construction details, junctions and installation conditions. Therefore, the website provides separate sections for wall and roof sandwich panels.

Can I order installation if the panels have already been purchased?

Yes, this option can be considered. To prepare a calculation, information will be required about the panels, the building, project documentation or dimensions, steel frame readiness and the required scope of work.

Can PANEL MONTAGE help with sandwich panel supply?

Yes. If the materials have not yet been purchased, panel selection and supply can be considered through a network of manufacturers and suppliers, taking into account the requirements of the specific project.

Can installation be calculated from drawings?

Yes. You can provide project documentation, drawings, sketches, dimensions and other available materials. Additional project parameters can be clarified where necessary.

Can sandwich panels be installed on an existing steel frame?

Such a project can be considered after the initial information has been received and the structure's readiness for installation of the building envelope has been assessed.

What can cause leaks or problems with panel joints?

Causes may include project solutions, condition of the supporting structure, construction details, junctions, fastening, sealing, accessories and the quality of installation work. For this reason, the complete system should be considered, not only the panel itself.

Does PANEL MONTAGE work throughout Kazakhstan?

Projects in different regions of Kazakhstan can be considered. Project location, logistics and conditions for organizing the work are taken into account when preparing the calculation.
