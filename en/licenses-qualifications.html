Licenses and Qualifications

For a construction project, material prices and installation costs are not the only important factors. Clients also need to understand who will perform the work, which documents are required and whether the contractor is qualified for the specific scope.

Request a Project Estimate Documentation Ask a Question
Working Principle

Documentation Must Match the Specific Type of Work

A construction project may involve several types of work: steel structures, building envelopes, roofing, facades, engineering systems and specialized solutions.

Contractor requirements are determined not by the name of the building or the contractor's advertising, but by the actual scope of work, the characteristics of the facility and applicable legal requirements.

When preparing a proposal, PANEL MONTAGE therefore considers the proposed scope of work, the arrangements for its execution and the documentation required.

License Categories

Why the License Category Matters

In Kazakhstan, the scope of construction and installation activities permitted to a license holder depends on the assigned category, the facility's level of responsibility, the conditions under which work is performed and the types of work covered by the license.

II

Category II

Legislation provides for work within the scope of the relevant license on facilities with the second and third levels of responsibility. On facilities with the first level of responsibility, certain works may be performed under subcontracting arrangements in cases provided for by law.

III

Category III

Legislation establishes a separate scope of permitted activities depending on the facility's level of responsibility and technical complexity, including the possibility of performing certain works under subcontracting arrangements.

The License Category Is Not the Only Factor to Verify

Holding a Category II or Category III license does not automatically authorize every type of construction work. For each facility, it is necessary to consider the license holder, license category, license appendix, authorized activities, facility characteristics and the actual scope of the contract.
