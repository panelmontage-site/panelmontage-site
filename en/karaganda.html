Karaganda and the Region

Industrial Buildings Must Meet Real Production Requirements

Karaganda Region is one of Kazakhstan's major industrial regions. It is home to metallurgical, mining, mechanical engineering, manufacturing and related industries.

These enterprises require production buildings, workshops, warehouses, repair facilities, hangars, technical buildings and engineering infrastructure.

PANEL MONTAGE approaches a facility not simply as a collection of panels, but as an integrated construction task involving the building's purpose, structural frame, building envelope, materials, installation and engineering systems.

Industrial Buildings

Define the Building's Purpose Before Selecting Its Structural Solution

An industrial facility cannot be properly planned based on floor area alone. It is essential to understand what activities will take place inside the building.

Equipment, internal workflows, loading doors, building height, temperature requirements, ventilation and engineering services can influence both the building's layout and its structural design.

The more accurate the initial information, the easier it is to determine the required materials and scope of installation work.

For an Industrial Facility

01 Type of production.
02 Main building dimensions.
03 Internal clear height.
04 Location of doors and openings.
05 Engineering requirements.
06 Current project stage.
Building Envelope

The Entire Building Envelope Matters for a Heated Facility

When selecting wall and roof systems, it is important to consider the intended use of the premises, design requirements and operating conditions.

Performance depends on more than the panels themselves. Joints, fasteners, corners, junctions, openings, flashing elements and installation quality are also important.

That is why PANEL MONTAGE considers materials together with their installation details, rather than treating them as isolated items on a purchasing list.

Steel Frame

Panel Installation Begins with Structural Readiness

Even high-quality panels can be difficult to install correctly if the steel frame geometry does not meet the actual installation requirements.

Before work begins, it is important to assess the condition of the structures, alignment, openings, junction locations and access to installation areas.

For an existing building undergoing reconstruction, an additional assessment of its actual condition may be required.

Checks Before Installation

01 Frame readiness.
02 Structural geometry.
03 Openings.
04 Supporting surfaces.
05 Access for lifting equipment.
06 Construction site conditions.
Engineering Systems

Ventilation Should Be Considered Before the Building Envelope Is Closed

In industrial and warehouse facilities, engineering systems are closely related to the purpose of the premises and the processes carried out inside them.

Air ducts, service penetrations, equipment and external components may intersect with the roof, walls and steel structures.

The earlier these interfaces are coordinated, the fewer modifications may be required after the building envelope has been installed.

Systems to Coordinate

02 Air conditioning.

04 Equipment placement.

Pricing

Cost Depends on the Facility, Not Just Its Square Meters

Two industrial buildings with the same floor area may require significantly different amounts of installation work.

Cost is influenced by building height, geometry, number of openings, frame type, materials, complexity of junction details, lifting equipment and site conditions.

It is therefore more accurate to establish the actual scope of work first and then prepare a commercial proposal.

Main Cost Factors

01 Area and scope of work.
02 Building height.
03 Building geometry.
04 Number of openings.
05 Required equipment.
06 Logistics and site conditions.
