Facade as a System

A Facade Is More Than Just a Cladding Panel

The appearance of a building depends on the selected material, but the performance of the facade is determined by the entire system: the substrate, substructure, fastening method, cladding geometry, treatment of corners, window and door openings, junctions and flashings.

Before purchasing materials, it is therefore important to understand the facade dimensions, architectural requirements, existing structures and installation method. This allows the material selection to be matched to the actual conditions of the project.

PANEL MONTAGE can become involved at the initial idea stage, during calculation, material selection or immediately before installation if the project and cladding have already been determined.

Facade Structure

What a Facade System Includes

The exact composition depends on the selected solution. When calculating a facade, it is important to consider not only the cladding area but also all elements required for installation on the specific building.

Substrate

The load-bearing part of the building to which the facade structure is connected.

Substructure

Its configuration is determined by the selected system, substrate and project solution.

Cladding

Facade panels, cassettes or another specified material.

Fasteners

Fastening elements are selected according to the structure and the facade system being used.

Openings

Window, door and gate openings require individual junction details.

Corners

External and internal corners affect the layout and quantity of additional elements.

Flashings

Used to finish joints, edges and junctions specified by the project.

Thermal Insulation

If included in the system, its parameters are determined by the technical solution for the project.

Additional Layers

Used only where they are specified by the selected system and project documentation.

Material Selection

Facade Panels Are Selected for the Specific Building

Appearance is important, but it is not the only consideration. Building dimensions, cladding module, architectural pattern, opening locations, fastening method and compatibility of system components must also be taken into account.

For industrial and commercial projects, practical issues are also important: delivery, unloading, storage, replacement of individual elements and future facade maintenance.

Installation experience helps assess material geometry, completeness, packaging and potential difficulties that may arise on site.

Frequently Asked Questions

Facade Panels and Facade Installation

What is the difference between facade panels and facade cassettes?

Facade panels are a broader category of exterior building cladding elements. Facade cassettes are a specific type of cladding with their own geometry, layout, fastening system and substructure.

Can a facade be calculated without a completed project?

Yes. A preliminary calculation can be started using building dimensions, elevations, photographs or sketches. Additional input data may be required for the final technical solution.

Can facade panels be ordered together with installation?

Yes. PANEL MONTAGE can consider material selection and supply coordination through a network of manufacturers and suppliers, as well as installation of the agreed facade system.

Can I order installation only?

Yes. If the material has already been purchased, the calculation requires information about the selected system, material volume, project documentation, condition of the substrate and project location.

Can a new facade be installed on an existing building?

The feasibility is determined after assessing the existing substrate, building geometry, condition of the structures and the selected facade system.

What determines the cost of a facade?

The cost depends on facade area and height, cladding type, substructure, building geometry, number of openings, corners and junctions, flashings, access conditions, material volume and project region.

Does PANEL MONTAGE work across Kazakhstan?

Yes. Projects in different regions of Kazakhstan are considered with regard to logistics, scope of work and the conditions of the specific site.
