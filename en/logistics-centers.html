More Than a Warehouse

A Logistics Center Is Designed Around the Flow of Goods

A conventional warehouse may primarily serve a storage function. A logistics center must additionally account for receiving, unloading, internal movement, order picking, dispatch and vehicle traffic.

Therefore, even before the final building structure is determined, it is useful to understand which operations will take place inside, what type of transport will serve the facility, what equipment is expected to be used and how storage will be organized.

If the facility currently exists only as an idea, the process can begin with concept development and definition of the initial project parameters.

Facility Calculation

Height, Spans and Loading Gates Directly Affect the Solution

Two logistics buildings with the same floor area can differ significantly in structural design and overall cost.

For example, increasing the building height, changing the spans, the number of loading gates, steel consumption and building envelope requirements changes the quantities of materials and installation work.

Therefore, a preliminary calculation is more useful when based on a specific set of initial parameters, rather than on floor area alone.

Warehouse Equipment

Storage Systems Should Be Considered Before the Building Is Completed

For a logistics center, usable capacity is determined not only by the floor area. Efficient use of building height and the organization of aisles are also important.

For this reason, the planned warehouse equipment should preferably be considered while the main building parameters are still being developed.

Through the storage systems and equipment direction, different racking solutions, metal cabinets and other items required for a particular facility can be considered.

Questions

Logistics Centers and Warehouse Complexes

Can we start if there is no project yet?

Yes. For the initial stage, you can provide information about the intended use of the facility, approximate area, height, number of loading gates, planned storage system and construction location.

How is a logistics center different from a conventional warehouse?

A logistics center is usually designed not only for storage, but also for receiving, internal movement, order picking and dispatch of goods. The layout therefore needs to take these processes into account.

Can you calculate a facility from an existing project?

Yes. Project documentation, drawings, specifications and bills of quantities can be used to calculate the relevant materials and installation works.

Can steel structure installation be ordered separately?

Yes. If the necessary project documentation and initial data are available, steel structure installation can be considered as a separate scope of work.

Can sandwich panel installation be ordered separately?

Yes. If the steel frame is ready and the required project data are available, wall and roof sandwich panel installation can be performed as a separate stage.

Can storage systems be selected for the facility?

Storage systems are included within the materials and equipment direction. The specific solution depends on warehouse parameters, the type of goods and the intended operating conditions.

Does PANEL MONTAGE work throughout Kazakhstan?

Yes. Projects are considered in different regions of Kazakhstan. Logistics, material supply and installation conditions are determined individually for each facility.
