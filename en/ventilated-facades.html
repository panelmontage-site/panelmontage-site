Facade System

A Ventilated Facade Is More Than Exterior Cladding

A ventilated facade should be considered as an interconnected system. The exterior cladding material is only its visible part.

The calculation must take into account the condition and geometry of the substrate, the configuration of the supporting substructure, cladding, fasteners, openings, corners, junctions and other building elements.

If a specific facade design includes thermal insulation or other additional layers, they should be considered together with the complete system rather than selected separately.

Cladding

Exterior Material Is Selected as Part of the Facade System

Cladding selection depends on the building architecture, facade dimensions, required geometry, desired appearance, operating conditions and compatibility between system components.

Some projects may use facade cassettes or other types of panel cladding. The specific solution is determined by the requirements of the project.

If the client has not yet selected a cladding type, we can first review the facade geometry and initial requirements, and then compare suitable options.

Frequently Asked Questions

Ventilated Facades

How is a ventilated facade different from ordinary cladding?

A ventilated facade is considered as a complete system that includes the substrate, supporting substructure, exterior cladding, fasteners and any additional elements required by the selected design.

Can a ventilated facade be calculated without a completed project?

Preliminary planning can begin using dimensions, facade drawings, photographs and a description of the building. Additional information may be required for the final solution.

What materials can be used for exterior cladding?

The cladding type is selected for the specific architectural and technical requirements of the project. Depending on the project, facade cassettes and other panel cladding solutions may be considered.

Can a ventilated facade be installed on an existing building?

This option can be considered, but the condition of the substrate, building geometry and the existing facade structure must first be taken into account.

Can materials and installation be ordered together?

Yes. PANEL MONTAGE can coordinate material selection and supply through a network of manufacturers and suppliers together with installation works.

What determines the cost of a ventilated facade?

The cost depends on the building area and height, cladding type, substructure, geometry, number of openings, components, substrate condition, installation conditions and logistics.

Does PANEL MONTAGE work only in Almaty?

No. Projects are considered in Almaty, Astana and other regions of Kazakhstan, taking into account the scope of work and logistics.
