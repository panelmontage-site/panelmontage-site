Practical Approach

Materials Are Evaluated with Their On-Site Installation in Mind

For installation work, the purchase price is not the only factor that matters. On site, practical issues become visible: product geometry, completeness, packaging condition, ease of installation and the quality of individual components.

Practical installation experience therefore helps us take a more careful approach to selecting materials and suppliers.

This does not mean that every defect can be identified in advance. However, installation practice provides additional information that cannot always be obtained from a commercial quotation alone.

What We Consider During Selection

✓ compliance with project parameters
✓ geometry and compatibility of components
✓ completeness of the supply package
✓ packaging and delivery conditions
✓ installation requirements
✓ related materials and fasteners

Questions

Materials and Supply

Can I order materials without installation?

Yes. Material selection and supply can be considered separately. The scope of supply is determined according to the requirements of the specific project.

Can I order both materials and installation?

Yes. Materials, supply and the corresponding installation works can be coordinated as consecutive stages within the same project.

Do you work with only one manufacturer?

No. PANEL MONTAGE considers different manufacturers and suppliers depending on technical requirements, availability and the conditions of the specific order.

Can you select materials based on an existing project?

Yes. Project documentation, specifications, bills of materials and drawings can be provided to determine the required materials and components.

What if I do not have a project yet?

You can start with the initial parameters of the facility. If necessary, we first determine whether a concept sketch, further project development or additional calculations are required.

Do you supply materials to regions across Kazakhstan?

Supply can be considered for projects in different regions of Kazakhstan. Conditions depend on the type of materials, order volume and project location.
