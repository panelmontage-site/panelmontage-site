Building Design Services

Warehouse, Hangar and Industrial Building Design

From initial requirements and the parameters of a proposed building to design solutions, cost estimation, material selection and subsequent project implementation.

You Can Start at Any Stage

You Do Not Need a Completed Design to Get Started

A complete set of drawings is not required for the initial enquiry. The important starting points are the intended use of the building, its preliminary parameters, the construction site and the desired outcome.

01

You Only Have an Idea

You know what type of building you need, but there is no concept design, technical brief or exact dimensions yet. The process begins with collecting initial information and defining the requirements.

02

You Have a Concept or Initial Information

The next scope of design work can be determined, the building parameters clarified and the information prepared for subsequent calculations.

03

You Already Have a Completed Design

Existing project documentation can be used to estimate materials, construction costs and installation work, select the required components and prepare a proposal for project implementation.

Building Types

Design Solutions Based on the Building's Intended Use

The scope and level of detail of design work depend on the building's purpose, dimensions, engineering systems, site conditions and project-specific requirements.

Warehouses

Warehouse buildings, distribution facilities and premises for storing goods and products.

Hangars

Prefabricated buildings for various purposes, based on steel structural systems.

Industrial Buildings

Workshops and manufacturing premises designed with the building's technological and operational requirements in mind.

Logistics Facilities

Warehouses and logistics complexes where layout, goods movement and operational efficiency are particularly important.

Commercial Buildings

Vehicle service centers, retail facilities, service premises and other business buildings.

Specialized Facilities

Cold storage rooms, healthcare facilities and other premises with additional technical requirements.

Project Workflow

From Initial Information to Project Implementation

We approach design as part of the complete project lifecycle, rather than as an isolated set of documents.

STAGE 01

Initial Information

Intended use, dimensions, site information, requirements and available documentation.

STAGE 02

Defining the Requirements

Determining the expected outcome and the scope of subsequent work.

STAGE 03

Building Design

Developing the necessary design solutions according to the project's requirements.

STAGE 04

Estimation and Procurement Planning

Using the design as the basis for estimating costs, materials and equipment requirements.

STAGE 05

Implementation

Material supply, construction and the relevant installation works.

Initial Enquiry

What Should You Send to Get Started?

There is no need to delay your enquiry until a complete technical specification is available. If the initial information is insufficient, the first step is to determine what additional details are needed for the next stage.

If you already have drawings, site photographs, an existing design, specifications or a cost estimate, these can be attached to your enquiry.

Submit Initial Information

Useful Initial Information

✓ Intended purpose of the building
✓ Construction region and location
✓ Approximate length, width and height
✓ Information about the land plot
✓ Available sketches, drawings or photographs
✓ Planned project timeline
✓ Special building requirements

Frequently Asked Questions

Building Design Services

Can I contact you if I only have an idea for a building?

Yes. The first stage can involve collecting initial information and determining which design solutions and documents will be required next.

Can I start with a preliminary concept design?

Yes. If the building concept, dimensions and appearance need to be developed first, the project can begin with the preliminary concept stage.

If I already have a design, can you estimate materials and installation costs?

Yes. Existing project documentation can serve as the basis for estimating materials, equipment, construction costs and the relevant installation works.

What files can I submit?

Through the estimate request page, you can submit available drawings, photographs, spreadsheets, design documents and other project materials.

Do you only work in Almaty?

No. Projects in Almaty, Astana, Shymkent, Karaganda, Atyrau, Aktau and other regions of Kazakhstan are considered.
