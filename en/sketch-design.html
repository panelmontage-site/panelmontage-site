Preliminary Building Concept Development

Concept Design for a Warehouse, Hangar or Industrial Building

If you do not yet have a complete construction design, you can begin by developing a preliminary concept for your future building: its purpose, dimensions, layout and appearance. This helps turn an initial idea into a clear starting point for subsequent design and cost estimation.

First Step

When You Have an Idea but No Building Design Yet

Often, a client already knows what type of building is needed: a warehouse, hangar, production facility, vehicle service center, cold storage room or commercial property — but does not yet have completed design documentation.

In this situation, immediately commissioning a full design may not always be the most practical starting point. The building's basic parameters and concept can first be developed at a preliminary level.

Concept design helps make discussions more specific, clarify the initial requirements and determine the appropriate next stage.

A Preliminary Concept Helps Define

✓ The overall building concept
✓ Approximate building dimensions

Concept Stage Deliverables

What Is Developed During Preliminary Concept Design?

The exact deliverables depend on the initial assignment. The purpose of this stage is to establish a clear preliminary understanding of the proposed building and provide a basis for the next decision.

01

Overall Concept

A basic understanding of the future building and its intended purpose.

02

Key Parameters

Approximate dimensions, floor area and overall building characteristics are clarified.

03

Preliminary Layout

The initial arrangement of major functional areas and building elements is defined.

04

Basis for the Next Stage

The concept can be used to discuss subsequent design requirements and cost estimation.

Important Distinction

A Preliminary Concept Is Not a Substitute for Detailed Design

Concept development is primarily intended to establish and visualize an initial building solution.

If the proposed building requires formal design documentation, structural calculations, engineering design sections or other documents under applicable requirements, these are developed during the appropriate subsequent stage.

Explore Detailed Building Design

Why Is Concept Design Useful?

It helps avoid starting detailed work with an undefined assignment. First, the client and relevant specialists establish a shared understanding of what is to be built, its approximate dimensions and its intended use. This makes it easier to determine the required scope of design, calculations and material selection.

Initial Information

What Information Should You Provide?

A complete set of project documents is not required for the initial enquiry. The more information you already have, the more precisely the assignment can be defined.

01

Building Purpose

Warehouse, hangar, production facility, vehicle service center, retail or another type of building.

02

Approximate Dimensions

Length, width, height or approximate floor area, if already known.

03

Project Location

The region and city or town where the proposed building will be located.

04

Site Information

Plot dimensions, photographs, site plans or any other available information.

05

Examples and Preferences

Photos of similar buildings, preferred exterior appearance or layout ideas.

06

Available Documents

Diagrams, drawings, technical requirements, specifications and other existing materials.

Frequently Asked Questions

Preliminary Building Concept Design

Can I request a concept design if I only have an idea?

Yes. Preliminary concept development helps turn an initial idea into a clearer building concept and identify the information required for further design.

Is a concept design a complete construction project?

No. Preliminary concept development and detailed construction design documentation are separate stages. Full design documentation is developed separately where required.

Can I order detailed design after the concept stage?

Yes. Once the concept has been clarified, the scope of subsequent design work can be determined.

Can the building cost be estimated after concept design?

The preliminary concept and updated project information can form the basis for an initial cost estimate. Accuracy depends on the amount and quality of available information.

Do you consider projects throughout Kazakhstan?

Yes. Projects in Almaty, Astana, Shymkent, Karaganda, Atyrau, Aktau and other regions of Kazakhstan are considered.
