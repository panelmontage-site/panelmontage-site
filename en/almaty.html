Sandwich Panels in Almaty

Walls and Roofing Should Be Considered as One Building System

When choosing sandwich panels, determining only the colour or the price per square metre is not enough.

It is important to consider the intended use of the premises, wall or roofing application, building geometry, connection details, junctions, openings, flashing elements and operating conditions.

Installation experience helps identify potential issues with geometry, component completeness, packaging, junctions and other common problems before they become apparent on site.

Cost

Why We Do Not Quote One Price per Square Metre for Every Project

Even buildings with identical floor areas may require substantially different quantities and types of work.

The cost depends on building height, geometry, panel type, number of openings, junctions, facade connections, lifting equipment, structural frame condition and site readiness.

We therefore determine the actual scope of work first, before preparing a project-specific proposal.

Factors Affecting the Cost

01 Area and height.
02 Building geometry.
03 Material type.
04 Openings and junctions.
05 Equipment and site access.
06 Actual installation quantities.
