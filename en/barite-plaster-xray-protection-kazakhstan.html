Purpose

Barite Plaster as Part of Permanent Radiation Shielding

Barite-containing construction mixtures are used to form protective coatings on walls and other building structures in rooms where the design specifies protection against X-ray radiation.

However, the product description "barite plaster" does not, by itself, establish whether the shielding is sufficient. The required coating properties must comply with the radiation protection calculations for the specific X-ray room.

For this reason, PANEL MONTAGE does not recommend a universal barite plaster thickness without the necessary project information and shielding design.

02 X-ray room layout.

Working Principle

Shielding Calculations First — Materials and Installation Second

Radiation-shielding layers cannot be selected solely on the basis of room area. The solution must be designed for the specific facility.

01

02

03

The purpose and occupancy of neighboring spaces are considered in the shielding calculation.

04

Shielding

The design specifies the required characteristics of permanent radiation-shielding structures.

05

Installation

Construction works are carried out according to the specified design solution.

There Is No Universal Barite Plaster Thickness

Required shielding levels may differ depending on the X-ray equipment, room layout and adjacent spaces. Therefore, coating thickness, lead equivalence and other shielding parameters must be determined from the radiation protection calculations and design documentation for the specific facility.

Important Details

Radiation Shielding Involves More Than Flat Wall Surfaces

Specialized construction works must account for corners, junctions, door and window openings, service penetrations and other locations where the continuity of the shielding system may be compromised.

Barite plaster does not automatically replace other radiation protection components specified in the design, including specialized doors, protective glazing, sheet shielding materials or alternative structural solutions.

The complete permanent shielding system is determined by the design for the specific X-ray room.

Frequently Asked Questions

Barite Plaster and Radiation Shielding

What is barite plaster used for?

Barite-containing construction mixtures may be used as part of permanent radiation shielding in rooms containing X-ray equipment when specified by the radiation protection calculation and design.

How thick should barite plaster be in an X-ray room?

There is no universal thickness suitable for every X-ray room. Shielding requirements depend on equipment characteristics, equipment positioning, building structures and adjacent areas.

Can barite plaster be installed according to an existing design?

Yes. An existing radiation protection design or calculation provides the basis for determining the scope of work, material quantities and installation method.

Can barite plaster completely replace lead shielding?

These materials should not be considered interchangeable without appropriate calculations. The type and specifications of the shielding structures are determined by the design for the specific room.

Can the cost be estimated from the room area?

A preliminary estimate requires the areas of protected surfaces, specified coating thickness, shielding system composition, substrate condition, number of layers, room height and site conditions.

Does PANEL MONTAGE work throughout Kazakhstan?

Yes. Specialized construction projects in Almaty, Astana and other regions of Kazakhstan are considered according to project scope and logistics.
