Cost Calculation

A Price per m² Without Project Parameters Can Be Misleading

Sandwich panel installation cannot be accurately estimated based only on the total area. The same 1,000 m² on two different buildings may require a different scope of preparation, lifting and installation operations.

One project may involve a simple wall with very few openings, while another may include a high structure, gates, windows, complex junctions, roofing and numerous flashing elements.

For this reason, PANEL MONTAGE calculates the cost according to the specific project, rather than only the number of square metres.

Project-Based Calculation

Why We Do Not Publish One Fixed Installation Price

A single price per square metre does not reflect the actual complexity of sandwich panel installation.

Two projects with the same panel area may differ significantly in building height, number of openings, roof configuration, access conditions, lifting requirements and the readiness of the supporting structure.

Therefore, a useful commercial calculation begins with the parameters of the specific facility.

Cost Questions

Sandwich Panel Installation Price

How much does sandwich panel installation cost per m²?

The cost depends on the parameters of the specific project: panel type, area, building height, building geometry, number of openings and junctions, material lifting conditions, readiness of the supporting frame and project location. A project calculation is required to determine a meaningful price.

Can I get a preliminary cost without a complete project?

Yes. To begin, you can provide the purpose of the building, city, length, width, height and approximate panel area. If necessary, PANEL MONTAGE specialists will request additional information.

Are the roof and walls calculated separately?

Yes. You can calculate wall panels only, roof panels only, or both types of installation work together.

Can I order installation only if the panels have already been purchased?

Yes. Send information about the purchased panels, the project or building dimensions, and information about the readiness of the supporting structures.

Can the panels and installation be calculated together?

Yes. If the materials have not yet been purchased, panel selection and supply, related components and installation work can be considered within one project.

Do you work only in Almaty?

No. PANEL MONTAGE considers projects in different regions of Kazakhstan. The calculation takes into account the project location, logistics and conditions for organizing the work.
