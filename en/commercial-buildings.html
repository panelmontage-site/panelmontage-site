Commercial Building Facade

Appearance Is Especially Important for a Commercial Building

While an industrial building is often evaluated primarily in terms of functionality, a commercial building interacts directly with customers and visitors.

The facade, entrance area, combination of materials and overall architecture become part of how the business is perceived.

For this reason, facade solutions should preferably be considered during the concept and design stages, rather than being added after the main structure is complete.

Budget

The Cost of a Commercial Building Depends on More Than Floor Area

A price per square meter without understanding the building configuration provides only a very rough estimate.

The final budget is affected by steel structures, facade systems, roofing, insulation, the number of openings, gates, engineering systems, selected materials and site conditions.

If the building needs to be evaluated before an investment decision is made, the process can begin with a preliminary calculation based on the available initial data.

A commercial building may combine several types of materials: sandwich panels, facade panels, facade cassettes, metal elements, fasteners, sealants and other components.

PANEL MONTAGE works with a network of manufacturers and suppliers, so materials can be considered in connection with the project and the future installation process.

This approach is especially useful when technical characteristics, the appearance of the building and the practicality of installation must all be considered together.

FAQ

Commercial Buildings

Can I start if I only have an idea for the future building?

Yes. You can describe the intended use of the building, approximate floor area, site, preferred dimensions and main requirements. It can then be determined which initial data are required for the next stage.

Do you work with stores and showrooms?

These types of commercial buildings can be considered in terms of design, calculations, structures, materials and the corresponding installation works.

Can you calculate the construction of an auto service center?

Yes. A preliminary calculation requires the main parameters: dimensions, height, number of gates, purpose of the work areas and project location.

Can facade works be ordered separately?

Yes. Facade panels, cassettes and other corresponding works can be considered as a separate stage of the project.

Can materials be selected and installation calculated at the same time?

Yes. If sufficient initial data are available, materials and the corresponding installation works can be considered together.

Do you work in different regions of Kazakhstan?

Yes. Projects are considered in different regions of Kazakhstan. Supply conditions, logistics and work arrangements are determined for each specific project.
