Logistics

A Warehouse Is Part of Cargo Flow, Not Just an Enclosed Space

A logistics facility requires consideration not only of building area but also vehicle movement, loading doors, loading and unloading zones, storage areas and internal cargo handling.

These factors can affect building geometry, opening locations, wall construction and the organization of installation works.

When submitting an enquiry, it is useful to explain how the future building is expected to operate.

For a Logistics Facility

01 Warehouse purpose.
02 Type of goods to be stored.
03 Building height.
04 Number and location of loading doors.
05 Loading and unloading zones.
06 Engineering requirements.
Materials and Operating Conditions

Materials Must Be Selected for the Specific Facility Conditions

When selecting building envelope and facade materials, the intended use, design requirements, operating conditions, frame construction, junction details and external envelope requirements must be considered.

In Aktau, it is particularly important not to select materials based solely on appearance or the lowest price. The solution should suit the actual project and its operating environment.

PANEL MONTAGE can consider materials together with the planned installation to account for supply completeness, fasteners, junctions and flashing elements at an early stage.

Material Selection Factors

01 Facility purpose.
02 Design requirements.
03 Operating conditions.
04 Wall or roof application.
05 Fasteners and junctions.
06 Completeness of the material supply.
Steel Frame

Frame Geometry Affects Subsequent Installation

Sandwich panels, facade elements and some engineering systems are installed in relation to existing building structures.

Deviations in the frame, unprepared openings or unresolved junction details can complicate subsequent installation.

Before work begins, it is therefore important to establish the actual readiness of the structures and construction site.

Before Installation Begins

01 Confirm frame readiness.
02 Clarify frame geometry.
03 Check openings.
04 Clarify junction details.
05 Determine equipment access.
06 Review site arrangements.
Material Supply

Material Deliveries Must Align with the Construction Schedule

When arranging deliveries, it is important to know when the site will be ready to receive materials, where they will be unloaded and how they will be moved to the installation area.

For long sandwich panels, steel components and facade materials, logistics becomes an integral part of site organization.

Linking estimation, materials, supply and installation helps reduce coordination gaps between the individual stages.

Delivery Planning Factors

01 Material quantities.
02 Component dimensions.
03 Packaging.
04 Unloading arrangements.
05 Storage location.
06 Installation sequence.

Can I request sandwich panel installation in Aktau?

You can submit an enquiry for wall and roof sandwich panel installation for industrial, warehouse, commercial or other facilities.

Can you estimate a warehouse or logistics facility?

Yes. To begin, provide the building purpose, dimensions, height, number of loading doors, intended operating arrangement and site location.

Projects outside Aktau can also be considered. Please provide the exact location so that logistics and site arrangements can be assessed.
