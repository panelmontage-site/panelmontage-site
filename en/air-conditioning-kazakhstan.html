System Selection

Air Conditioning Is Selected Using More Than Floor Area Alone

Floor area is only one of the initial parameters. For a commercial or industrial facility, room volume, intended use, number of occupants, operating schedule, glazing, equipment and sources of heat gain should also be considered.

In larger buildings, individual rooms may operate under different conditions. Therefore, instead of selecting a random set of equipment, it is more practical to first define the zones and the requirements for each one.

If the facility is still at the design stage, equipment locations and engineering routes are better considered in advance.

Ventilation + Air Conditioning

Two Different Systems Should Work Together

Air conditioning and ventilation perform different functions. Having an air conditioner does not by itself mean that the required air exchange has been organized.

If a facility requires both systems, they should preferably be considered together: equipment locations, service routes, ductwork, penetrations through building structures and access for future maintenance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Air Conditioning for Facilities

Can air conditioning be selected based only on floor area?

Floor area is useful for a preliminary estimate, but for commercial and industrial facilities it is also advisable to consider room height, intended use, occupants, equipment, glazing and other factors.

Can equipment supply and installation be ordered together?

Yes. Equipment selection, system completion, supply coordination and installation can be considered as one integrated task.

Is ventilation necessary if the building has air conditioning?

These are different engineering systems. Air conditioning should not automatically be considered a substitute for organized ventilation. The requirements depend on the specific purpose of the space.

Can an air conditioning system be installed in an existing building?

Yes. The layout, possible service routes, equipment locations and existing engineering constraints should first be assessed.

What determines the installation cost?

The cost depends on the equipment, number of rooms and zones, length of service routes, installation height, access complexity, condition of the facility and project region.

Do you work throughout Kazakhstan?

Yes. Projects are considered in Almaty, Astana and other regions of Kazakhstan, taking into account the scope of work and logistics.
