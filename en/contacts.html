Project Information

What to Send for a Meaningful Estimate

The more accurate the initial information, the fewer assumptions are needed when determining materials, quantities and installation work.

If the Building Has Not Yet Been Constructed

You can start with the basic parameters.

01City and location.
02Intended use of the building.
03Approximate length and width.
04Required height.
05Expected temperature conditions.
06Operational requirements of the building.

If the Building Already Exists

Send any available documentation and actual site information.

01Photographs of the facility.
02Design documents or drawings.
03Material specifications.
04Actual dimensions.
05Condition of the steel frame.
06List of required works.
After Your Inquiry

How a Project Inquiry Is Processed

01 / INQUIRY

Receive Information

Identify the project location and the client's requirements.

02 / CLARIFICATION

Review the Initial Information

Clarify dimensions, materials, structural details and the current project stage where necessary.

03 / ESTIMATION

Determine the Scope

Consider materials, supply packages, installation and related work.

04 / NEXT STEPS

Agree on the Next Stage

Determine whether design, supply, installation or another project stage is required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Contacting PANEL MONTAGE

What is the quickest way to receive an initial response?

For a construction project, send a WhatsApp message with the city, intended use, dimensions and any available photographs or drawings.

Can I send my project documents by email?

Yes. You can send project documents and supporting materials to montaj.panel.kz@gmail.com.

Can I contact you for materials only?

Yes. If installation has already been arranged, you can request assistance with material selection, preparation of a supply package and delivery coordination.

Can I request installation services only?

Yes. If you already have a design and materials, you can send the documentation to determine the scope of installation work.

Do you consider projects outside Almaty?

Yes. Inquiries are considered for projects in different regions of Kazakhstan. Please specify the exact location of the construction site.

What if I do not have exact dimensions yet?

Provide the intended use of the building, region, approximate dimensions and current project stage. This will help identify the additional information needed.
